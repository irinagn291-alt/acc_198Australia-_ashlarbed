Ashlarbed is a personal lift log. You write a set on the Tower, watch today’s scaffold rise, then Bed the session to fold that tonnage into climbable 500 kg floors.

• Tower: pick a lift, enter Weight and Reps, then Write the set onto today’s open scaffold
• Standard lifts: Back squat, Bench press, Deadlift, Overhead press; add, rename or remove lifts in Settings
• Today’s scaffold shows Live tonnage, Floors waiting, Carry and each logged set
• Bed the session writes 500 kg storeys, carries remainder under 500 kg into tomorrow, and scores the day against the daily floor goal
• Bedding with no sets writes a Rest band
• A PR stamp appears only when you beat a Weight or an Epley 1RM
• Analytics: Bedded floors, Volume and Earned PR stamps
• Scaffold, then bed explains that sets never skip the scaffold
• Settings: Lifts, Experience and aim, Sources, Contact Ashlarbed, Re-run onboarding, Reset all data
• Sets, floors and stamps stay on this device

Daily floor goal is experience times aim. This is a personal training log, not medical advice.
